Hello! I am a PhD candidate in applied economics at the University of Illinois Chicago, and I am on the 2026–2027 economics job market.

My research focuses on the economics of education, particularly school accountability and how families respond to information about school quality. I study how language barriers and differences in access to information shape school choices. My broader interests include labor and development economics.

Prior to starting the PhD, I spent five years at the Institute for Competitiveness in Gurgaon, first as a data analyst and then as a research manager, leading economic and policy research.
