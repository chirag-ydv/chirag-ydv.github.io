PhD candidate in Applied Economics, University of Illinois Chicago

Hello! I am a PhD candidate in applied economics at the University of Illinois Chicago.My research is primarily in economics of education, with a focus on school accountability and public information. I am particularly interested in how families respond to information about school quality, and how language and access barriers shape who is able to act on it. I am also deeply interested in topics in labor and development economics.

I am on the 2026-2027 economics job market.

Prior to starting the PhD, I spent five years at the Institute for Competitiveness in Gurgaon, first as a data analyst and then as a research manager, leading economic and policy research for government and private-sector clients.
